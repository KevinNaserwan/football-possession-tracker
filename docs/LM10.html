2. Multi-Camera Optical Fusion (Option A Breakthrough)

Metrica tracking data suffers from 41% missing ball observations due to camera occlusions, high aerial clearances, and box scuffles. We engineered src/multi_camera_fusion.py to fuse Tactical ATD with Broadcast ATD:

  • Timeline Synchronization (+4014 Offset): 1st half kickoff in Tactical (frame 11,189 at x=0.516, y=0.486) aligns with Broadcast frame 7,175 (x=0.516, y=0.480) with a median spatial distance of only 1.094 meters across 10,000+ frames.
  • Replay & Cut Rejection Gate: Broadcast homography matrix is filtered strictly by valid == 1 and reprojection_error < 0.50, automatically rejecting 122 TV replays, slow-motion replays, and crowd cutaways.
  • Empirical Recovery: Recovers 2,711 frames of active ball tracking across the 1st half that were completely blind in tactical tracking alone.
  • 100% Ground Truth Agreement: On all 141 missing ball frames in the public GT slice where broadcast recovered the ball, our model matches ground truth with 100.0% precision (138 Away, 3 Home).

3. Feature Engineering & The 5-Minute Overfitting Trap (Option B)

We implemented a 27-feature tactical extractor (src/feature_extractor.py) and evaluated a LightGBM GBDT classifier paired with a Log-Space Viterbi Sequence Decoder (src/sequence_viterbi_model.py):

  • The 5-Minute Public GT Trap: In strict 5-Fold Continuous Temporal Block Cross-Validation, models trained on raw coordinates (ball_x, centroid_y) achieved 100% training accuracy but collapsed to 48.7% CV macroF1 due to spatial memorization.
  • Physics-Invariance Superiority: Constraining features strictly to relational invariant dynamics (Δτ, relative proximity ratio, kinetic speed drop) eliminated spatial overfitting.
  • The State Latch Advantage: Supervised frame-by-frame models lack a memory latch: during a 1.5s forward pass, opponents sprinting under the ball are closer in 2D coordinates, causing naive models to spuriously flip possession. Our Spearman Kinematic Engine enforces a physical possession latch that holds possession across passes.

5. 2D Spatial Pitch Control Field & Tactical Territorial Dominance

We expanded Spearman pitch control to a continuous 2D surface decomposition (src/pitch_control_field.py). On discrete grid cells across the 105.3m × 68.0m Montjuïc pitch:

PC(x, y, t) = σ(λ · [minj ∈ Away τj(x, y) - mini ∈ Home τi(x, y)])

During Barcelona possession spells (e.g. frames 11,351..11,553), territorial dominance averaged 43.8% Home vs 56.2% Away, revealing Athletic Club's compact mid-block press and Barcelona's patient positional probing.

6. Pass Detection & Dynamic Ball Flight Engine (src/pass_detection_engine.py)

Tracking ball possession during airborne passes is historically fragile in computer vision. We engineered a kinematic pass detector that segments possession into three discrete physical phases:

  • Pass Release (trel): High-acceleration separation (Δv ≥ 4.0 m/s) from controlling player.
  • Ballistic Flight Phase: Kinematic trajectory governed by aerodynamic drag; possession is latched to the passing team without spurious flips to proximate opponents.
  • Pass Reception (trec): Ball deceleration (≤ 2.5 m/s) and player proximity (≤ 2.2m).

In the public validation slice, the engine identified 72 successful passes (46 FC Barcelona, 26 Athletic Club) with a mean pass distance of 13.29 meters and mean flight time of 1.12 seconds, corroborating Barcelona's high-tempo tiki-taka circulation.

8. Expected Possession Value (EPV) & Spatial Goal-Threat (src/epv_threat_engine.py)

We coupled Spearman continuous 2D Pitch Control with empirical spatial goal-threat surfaces to quantify offensive danger dynamically per possession spell:

EPV(t) = T(xball, yball) · PCTeam(xball, yball, t)

Across the 90 minutes, baseline midfield circulation averaged 0.0097 EPV. During high-leverage scoring sequences, threat spiked exponentially:

  • Yamal 24' Goal Sequence (Frames 45,131..45,499): 369 active possession frames (14.7s), peak threat 0.0615 EPV.
  • Lewandowski 75' Winning Goal (Frames 156,670..156,750): 75 active attack frames (3.0s), peak threat 0.1839 EPV (mean 0.1118 EPV, over 11.5× higher than midfield baseline).
