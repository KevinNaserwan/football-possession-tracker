1. Problem Statement & The spellF1 Metric Challenge

The Initial Challenge requires classifying all 199,319 frames of FC Barcelona vs. Athletic Club into four discrete states: H (Barcelona in possession), A (Athletic Club in possession), D (Dead Ball), or X (Abstention: pre-match, halftime gap, post-match).

The official ranking metric is spellF1, defined as the mean F1 score across five temporal Intersection-over-Union (IoU) matching thresholds (θ ∈ {0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7}). Standard per-frame distance heuristics produce catastrophic fragmentation: nearest-player algorithms predict 131 disjoint possession spells (vs. 17 true possessions), scoring a trivial spellF1 = 0.0378 despite an apparent macroF1 = 0.5972. To win on spellF1, possession must be modeled as a continuous physical state with temporal inertia.

2. Methodology & Mathematical Architecture

2.1 Spearman Interception Surface (Δt)

For ball position rb at frame t, each tracked player j with position rj and velocity vj requires arrival time tj:

rj,react = rj + vj · τreact  |  dj = ||rj,react - rb||2  |  tj = τreact + dj / vmax

where τreact = 0.5s (neuro-muscular reaction latency) and vmax = 7.5 m/s (maximum sprint speed). Team possession probability is governed by the logistic differential P(Home) = 1 / (1 + exp(-λ (tA* - tH*))) with sharpness λ = 4.3.

2.2 The 41% Missing Ball Trap & Possession Latch

The ball is absent (NaN) on 41.0% of graded gameplay frames due to aerial clearances, penalty-box scuffles, and camera occlusions. While naive baselines default to D (causing severe possession splitting), our Possession Latch maintains continuous team possession across occlusions and passing trajectories unless a verified stoppage occurs.

2.3 Turnover Control Radius (≤ 2.2m) & Zero-Phase Backdating

Under competition rules, deflections without control do not transfer possession. We enforce:

  • Physical Reach Guard (dopp ≤ 2.2m): Opponents cannot initiate a turnover while the ball is traveling in the air or cleared downfield (d > 2.2m).
  • Confirmation Window (K = 12 frames, 0.48s): Requires sustained control superiority before flipping state.
  • Zero-Phase Backdating: Once confirmed, the transition timestamp is recorded at frame t - K + 1 (initial physical contact), cutting mean latency from +34 frames to -2.3 frames (0.09s) and achieving 100% transition recall (20/20 matched).

2.4 Multi-Modal Stoppage Segmentation (Dead Ball D)

Play is marked as D when: (1) ball crosses pitch boundaries (x < -0.01, x > 1.01, y < -0.01, y > 1.01), (2) a pre-restart set-piece interval is active (expanded 150 frames before restart to capture the preceding foul/whistle), or (3) collective player speed collapses (vmean < 1.15 m/s, vmax < 2.80 m/s) outside active tactical phases.

3. Engineering Trade-Offs (Deliberately Dropped Assets)

Dropped Component Initial Purpose Technical Justification for Dropping Engineering Benefit
Raw Video Pixel Decoding (.mp4) YOLO / ByteTrack optical tracking Decoding 199,319 1080p frames requires ~350 GB I/O and >6h compute. ATD stream already provides 25 fps centroids at 0.05m accuracy. Inference runtime dropped from 6 hours to 6.10 seconds on CPU.
Broadcast Stream (BAR-ATH_broadcast) Multi-view perspective fusion Broadcast video features pan/zoom cuts and partial player visibility (10–14 players). Tactical camera continuously covers all 22 players. Eliminated camera-sync drift; preserved 100% planar coordinate consistency.
Deep Sequence Models (Bi-LSTM / Transformers) Learned temporal state transitions Public ground truth contains only 5 minutes of labels. Deep networks overfit this small slice and fail on the unobserved 85 minutes. Physics-grounded model has zero unconstrained weights, ensuring robust full-match generalization.
