1. Executive Summary & Approach

The Initial Challenge of More Than A Hack 2027 tasks participants with predicting frame-by-frame ball possession (H for FC Barcelona, A for Athletic Club, D for Dead Ball, or X for abstention) across all 199,319 frames of an official LaLiga match recorded at 25 fps.

The competition's official ranking metric is spellF1—the mean possession-spell F1 score evaluated across five temporal Intersection-over-Union (IoU) thresholds (0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7). Naive frame-by-frame classifiers fail on this task because high-frequency touch fluctuations split continuous possessions into hundreds of disconnected fragments, dropping spellF1 toward zero (e.g., baseline_raw achieves spellF1 = 0.0378).

Team LM10 developed a physics-grounded, zero-phase pitch control engine that models possession as a continuous physical state governed by:

  • Spearman Spatial Pitch Control & Time-to-Intercept (Δt): Quantifies ball control via sprint kinematics, reaction latency (0.5s), and pitch geometry.
  • Turnover Control Radius Guard (≤ 2.2m): Forbids possession changes when the ball is in flight, cleared, or in transit without an opponent in physical reach.
  • Smart Tagging Tactical Fusion: Aligns 59 set-piece annotations with kinematic stoppage detection while using active play tags (PROGRESSION, BUILD UP, ATTACKING TRANSITION) to prevent false dead-ball detections during ball occlusions.
  • Zero-Phase Backdated Transition Latching: Eliminates detection phase lag by backdating confirmed turnovers to the exact initial contact frame, achieving a near-zero mean latency of -2.3 frames (0.09s).

2. Asset Usage & Engineering Trade-Offs

2.1 Assets Utilized

  • BAR-ATH_tactical_atd.txt & .xml: Primary stream. 199,319 lines of 25 fps coordinates and velocities for 22 players and the ball on a 105.3m × 68.0m pitch.
  • BAR-ATH_tactical_smart_tagging.xml: 638 SportsCode tactical instances providing high-fidelity set-piece restart priors and active play phases.
  • BAR-ATH_tactical_homography.csv: 3×3 planar transformation matrix verifying pitch boundaries and goal lines.

2.2 Deliberately Dropped Assets (Senior Development Principles)

  • Raw Video Pixel Decoding (.mp4): Skipped to avoid massive disk I/O and multi-hour GPU decoding. The optical ATD tracking stream already contains 25 fps centroids. Dropping video pixels reduced our full match runtime to 6.10 seconds without accuracy loss.
  • Broadcast Camera Stream: Skipped because broadcast video features frequent close-up cuts, replays, and partial player visibility (10–14 players). The Tactical feed continuously covers all 22 players and the complete pitch.
  • Black-Box Deep Neural Networks (e.g. Bi-LSTM / Transformers): Skipped because the public training ground truth is only 5 minutes. High-capacity neural networks easily suffer from catastrophic domain overfitting on this small sample, whereas our physics-grounded Spearman solver generalizes deterministically across the full 90 minutes.

3. Mathematical Formulation & Possession Rules

3.1 Spearman Interception Surface

For ball coordinate rb at frame t, each tracked player j with position rj and velocity vj requires an interception time:

rj,react = rj + vj · τreact  |  dj = ||rj,react - rb||2  |  tj = τreact + dj / vmax

where τreact = 0.5s and vmax = 7.5 m/s. Possession probability follows the logistic differential:

P(Home) = 1 / (1 + exp(-λ (tA* - tH*))),   λ = 4.3

3.2 Turnover Control Radius & Anti-Deflection Rule

Per official competition rules, "a deflection or simple touch without control does NOT count as a change of possession". We enforce:

  1. Physical Control Radius (d ≤ 2.2m): A candidate turnover is only considered if the opponent is within tackling/control range. Long balls, aerial clearances, and forward passes in flight cannot transfer possession.
  2. Hysteresis Confirmation Window (K = 12 frames): The challenger must maintain control superiority for at least 0.48s.
  3. Zero-Phase Backdating: Once confirmed, the transition timestamp is recorded at the initial touch frame (t - K), eliminating transition lag.
